# Field Interaction Round 1: Questionnaire & Discussion Guide

**Project:** Household Appliance Energy Use Forecasting (BCP308 Mini Project)  
**Target Participants:** 6–8 households (family members, neighbours, hostel/PG residents)  
**Format:** Informal 10-minute conversation | **Confidentiality:** Anonymous by default unless consent is given.

---

### Introduction Script (Read to participant)
> *"Hi! Thank you for taking 10 minutes to help with my college project. I am studying how households manage electricity and whether smart alerts can help people save energy. There are no right or wrong answers, and your name will remain confidential unless you give consent. May I ask you a few quick questions?"*

---

### Questions

1. **Tariff Awareness:**  
   Do you know how your electricity is billed (e.g., flat monthly rate, tiered slabs by total units, or peak vs. off-peak rates)? Do you check your bill breakdown or just pay the final amount?

2. **Peak Usage Awareness:**  
   During a normal day, do you know which hours your household consumes the most electricity? What clues tell you that usage is high (e.g., specific appliances running, noise, bill surprises)?

3. **Routine vs. Flexibility:**  
   Which major electrical appliances run at fixed times due to work or school schedules, and which ones could theoretically be run earlier or later in the day?

4. **Willingness to Shift Usage:**  
   If you received an advance warning that electricity demand (or tariff rate) would be high over the next hour, would you actually postpone running flexible appliances? Why or why not?

5. **Advance Notice Requirement:**  
   How much advance notice would you realistically need to delay running an appliance—10 minutes, 1 hour, or a notice the previous night?

6. **Preferred Alert Format (Pick One):**  
   Which alert format would you actually pay attention to and act on?
   - **Option A (Numeric):** An hourly prediction number (e.g., *"Expected demand: 180 Wh"*).
   - **Option B (Window Warning):** A clear traffic-light window warning (e.g., *"High usage window ahead between 6:00 PM and 8:00 PM — please shift flexible loads"*).
   - **Option C (Daily Summary):** A single end-of-day summary comparing today's consumption against yesterday.

7. **Main Barrier to Action:**  
   If you received an alert, what would be the single biggest reason you might ignore it (e.g., inconvenience, forgetting, lack of financial incentive, family members not cooperating)?

8. **Preferred Communication Channel:**  
   What channel would you prefer for an alert—a WhatsApp text, a mobile app push notification, or an SMS message?

9. **Motivation Factor:**  
   Would your primary motivation to shift consumption be lowering your monthly bill, preventing local grid overload/power cuts, or reducing environmental impact?

10. **Follow-Up Consent (Round 2):**  
    Once I finish building the prediction model and small prototype app, would you be willing to look at a 2-minute demo and tell me if the output is helpful?
