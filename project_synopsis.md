# Project 26: Forecasting Household Appliance Energy Use

## Abstract

Households cannot shift load if they cannot anticipate it, and tariff or
supply schemes that reward shifting therefore go unused. This project
forecasts appliance energy consumption from indoor temperature and
humidity sensors plus outdoor weather, at ten-minute resolution. Lag and
time-of-day features are compared against a persistence baseline, and
the honest finding is usually how hard this target is to predict.

## Community Development Rationale

The community is households facing rising tariffs with no visibility of
their own consumption pattern. Field contact: households on whether they
would actually shift usage given a forecast, because a prediction nobody
acts on delivers no saving regardless of its accuracy.

## Dataset

**UCI 374 Appliances Energy Prediction** --- 19,735 records, 29
features.

Dataset:
